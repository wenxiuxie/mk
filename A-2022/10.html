Problem

每个篮子里都有一只动物在睡觉。考拉和狐狸睡在图案和形状都相同的篮子里。袋鼠和鸵鸟睡在图案相同的篮子里。小狗睡在哪个篮子里？

One animal sleeps in each basket. The koala and fox are in baskets with the same pattern and shape. The kangaroo and ostrich share the same pattern. Which basket is the puppy in?

实验：把动物放进篮子

Lab: place animals in baskets

点选动物，再点篮子。相同图案/形状的篮子会高亮。推出小狗的篮子后选 A–E（1–5 号篮）。

Pick an animal, tap a basket. Matching pairs highlight. Deduce the puppy's basket, then pick A–E (baskets 1–5).
