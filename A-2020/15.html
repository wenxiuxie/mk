题目

Problem

卡片在桌上。先沿顶边翻转，再沿左边翻转。现在卡片是什么样？

The card is on the table. Flip it over its top edge, then over its left edge. What does it look like now?

实验台：跟着图案翻两次

Lab: flip the real pattern twice

① 沿顶边翻 = 上下翻转（三角形尖朝向会变）
② 再沿左边翻 = 左右翻转（顺序左右对调）
① Over the top = vertical flip (triangle tip flips)
② Over the left = horizontal flip (left↔right order)
