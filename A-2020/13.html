Largest Shade

题目

Problem

以下哪张图片中的阴影部分面积最大？

Which picture has the largest shaded area?

实验台：别数灰的——数白的更快

Lab: don’t count grey — count white

每张图都是 3×3 = 9 格。
阴影格数 = 9 − 白色格数（半格白算 0.5）
白越少 → 阴影越大。只要找出「白最少」的那张！
Each picture is a 3×3 = 9 grid.
Shade = 9 − white (a half-white counts as 0.5).
Less white → more shade. Find the one with the least white!
1
只盯着白色（整格白 / 三角形半格白）
2
给每张图记下白色多少
3
看下方「阴影条」谁最长
4
选出阴影最大的选项
1
Only look at white (full or half triangles)
2
Tally white for each picture
3
Longest shade bar wins
4
Pick that option

点「+½ 白」或「+1 白」记录；半格三角形点一次，整格白点「+1」。

Tap +½ white or +1 white. One triangle = half; a full white square = +1.

阴影长度赛跑（自动 = 9 − 白）

Shade race (auto = 9 − white)

小提示：半格白像「三角形」；两块半格白 = 1 格白。不必把灰色一格格加起来。

Tip: a white triangle is half a cell; two halves = one. No need to add every grey cell.
