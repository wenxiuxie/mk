Problem

Mara 用下面五块形状中的 四块 拼成了正方形。哪一块没有被用到？

Mara built the square using four of the five shapes. Which shape was not used?

实验台：先数图案，再凑四块

Lab: count symbols, then pick four pieces

1
点大正方形里的格子，数清每种图案有几个（★ ◆ ○ →）。 Tap cells in the big square and count each symbol (★ ◆ ○ →).
2
再点下面的 A–E：选中 =「我要用这块」。要选满 4 块，且图案个数加起来要和大正方形一样。 Tap A–E to mark pieces as used. Choose exactly 4 pieces whose symbol totals match the square.
3
剩下没选中的那一块，就是「没用到」的答案。 The one piece left out is the unused shape.
