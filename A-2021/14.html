Julie 和 Angela 玩一种叫做 “kangball” 的球类游戏，每进一球得 2 分。Julie 进了 5 个球，Angela 进了 9 个球。请问 Angela 比 Julie 多得几分？

Julie and Angela played “kangball”. Each goal scores 2 points. Julie scored 5 goals and Angela scored 9 goals. How many more points than Julie did Angela score?

选项：(A) 4　(B) 6　(C) 8　(D) 10　(E) 12
Choices: (A) 4　(B) 6　(C) 8　(D) 10　(E) 12

实验台：先摆进球，再算分数差

Lab: place goals, then compare points
