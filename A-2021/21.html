题目

Problem

三个女孩和两个男孩跳舞。他们两人一对，每个女孩与每个男孩都正好跳一分钟。任意时刻场上只有一对。他们一共跳了多少分钟？

Three girls and two boys danced in pairs so each girl danced with each boy for exactly one minute. Only one pair at a time. For how many minutes did they dance?

实验台：一对对上场跳

Lab: send pairs onto the floor

1
点下面一对名字，看他们走到舞台中央跳舞（每次只有一对）。Tap a pair — they walk to center and dance (only one pair at a time).
2
每跳完一对，时钟 +1 分钟。把所有配对都跳完。Each finished pair adds 1 minute. Dance every pair.
3
看总分钟数，再选题。Read the total minutes, then pick an answer.
